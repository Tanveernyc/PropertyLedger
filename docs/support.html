Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the dashboard, and tap Delete account in the footer. You'll be asked to type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account and every property, expense, income record, and custom category you created. It cannot be undone, so export your data first if you want to keep a copy.

How do I export my records?

From the dashboard, tap Export. The app produces a CSV of your records and opens the share sheet, so you can email it, save it to Files, or send it to your accountant.

A bill covers a different year than when I paid it. What do I do?

When adding an expense, fill in the optional period start and end dates. PropertyLedger records both when the money moved and the span the bill covers, so a school tax bill paid in September for the following year lands in the right place in your reports.

I sold a property. Will I lose its history?

No. Archive the property instead of deleting it — the records stay intact for your tax return, and the property stops appearing in your active lists.
